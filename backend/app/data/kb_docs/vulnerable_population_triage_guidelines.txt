MINISTRY OF HOME AFFAIRS / NDMA RELIEF ADVISORY
VULNERABLE POPULATION IDENTIFICATION, ACCESSIBILITY TRIAGE & SPECIALIZED CARE
Document Ref: NDMA/VULN-TRG/2024/03 | Classification: Operational Directive

1. DEFINITION OF HIGH VULNERABILITY COHORTS
In disaster vulnerability assessments, vulnerable individuals are counted as:
- Elderly citizens (aged 65 and above)
- Children (aged 0 to 12 years)
- Persons with disabilities (locomotor, visual, cognitive, hearing)
- Individuals with chronic medical conditions requiring daily electricity/medication (insulin, dialysate, oxygen)

2. VEHICLE ALLOCATION METRIC
For habitations where vulnerable population percentage exceeds 25%:
- The ratio of ambulance / low-floor wheelchair accessible vans to standard passenger buses must be at least 1:3.
- Paramedic personnel must accompany each evacuation convoy departing from Critical habitations.

3. RECEPTION & SHELTER ZONING FOR SPECIAL CARE
- Shelters accepting high vulnerable populations must have designated ground-floor barrier-free zones.
- Adequate stock of pediatric oral rehydration salts (ORS), geriatric medication kits, and baby formula must be mobilized within 2 hours of shelter activation.
